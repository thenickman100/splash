Titans Softball

The first site someone asked me to build.

Startedc. 2010
Addresssoftball.askthenickman.com
Commissioned byMy dad

Commission

My dad coached my sister's softball team. He likes sports and is very into stats. He wanted one place where parents on the team could get information on practices and games, see photos from the games, and check their kids' stats. It was the first site anyone asked me to build, and because it was built to his specifications, I learned how to work with a customer to find out what they need. I built it shortly before the first season, and it lived at the subdomain softball.askthenickman.com.

Rebuilt site

Click through the tabs along the top. To protect the players' privacy, the team leaders are shown by their initials. The roster and stat sheets had the players' full names and their families' phone numbers, so I left them out, and those buttons no longer go anywhere. The photo albums were on an outside photo site and aren't included. The home page played “Centerfield” when it opened.
