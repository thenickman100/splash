EnGen NextGen

My second commission, paid for in personal training.

Started2014
Addressengennextgen.org
Commissioned byThe club's founder

Commission

I had to get in shape for basic training. My best friend had a friend who was very fit, so I asked him to be my personal trainer. He had a girlfriend who wanted a website, and he said he'd train me if I made it. She wanted to build her resume for college, so she was starting a club called EnGen NextGen, the next generation of engineers, which would meet weekly with professional engineers. I worked hard to make the site engineering themed. The front page showcased the club, which I joined. We did photo shoots for the site's photos, someone wrote blog entries that I uploaded, and there was a calendar of the club's events. The club was mostly there to help people get into college, so it didn't last.

Competition

At the same time, I was in a drafting class in high school, and my teacher signed everyone up for engineering competitions at the local college. Web design was one of the categories, so I entered the club's site. I kept working on it all the way up to the competition, and it won first place.

Domain expiry

Later I used the site for my own blog, where the club's blog had been. I had a communications class where we had to keep a blog and were graded on our posts, and I hosted mine on engennextgen.org. When the domain expired around 2015, everything on it was lost, including the version I worked so hard on and entered in the competition. I think I kept all the files on the web host and never saved them on my own computer. The only code that survived is the first version, from August 2014, saved on an old backup drive along with two of my class posts.

Rebuilt site

Click through the tabs, and click the pictures on the home page to open them. The posts and the calendar were stored in a database that was lost with the domain. The site now runs on the files that survived, which are one of the club's blog posts, two of my class posts, the About text, and the photos from our shoots. I rebuilt the layout from my memory of the version I entered in the competition.
