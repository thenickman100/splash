First version

I built the first version in November 2016 with PHP and jQuery. Every time I opened a browser, it greeted me with “Welcome Nick” and the date and time, and filled in from RSS news feeds, Yahoo stock data, weather APIs, Reddit, and more. Whenever one of those APIs changed, the whole site crashed. A friend saw the page and was impressed with it, and he later picked me to lead the web design on Space Atlas.

Rebuilt first version

The first version, running on the data it had saved by May 2018. Click a stock ticker to see its chart. The orange tab showed Reddit, and because no Reddit posts were saved, it now shows a saved Defense News feed. Nothing is downloaded or saved, and the homework board can't be changed.

Second version

After too many crashes, I rebuilt the page with every feed in its own module. Each module was a bubble that expanded when you opened it. It looked cleaner but was less interesting. The bubbles held the same feeds as before, plus my website bookmarks with a bubble for each category, more detailed stock data you could interact with, and notes. When I got married in 2019, I added a module to manage, track, and visualize our spending and finances. I still use it, mostly for finances.
