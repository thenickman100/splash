Hack a HOBO

Started 2011hackahobo.infoEvolved from Askthenickman

My sister came up with the name. I wanted the site themed around cavemen, and I thought hobo was a word for a caveman. Hack probably came from the Awesome Stuff era of hacking tutorials. When I moved to Hack a HOBO in early 2011, the layout was inspired by Facebook’s design at the time.

Open full screen ↗

Hack a HOBO started when I was in middle school and continued through my high school years as a way for kids to get around blocked gaming sites at school.

Profiles

At some point I started learning PHP and wanted to make my own social media site, so I added profiles to Hack a HOBO. It was hard to prevent spamming and injection attacks, so eventually I got rid of profiles. I never liked gaming sites that tried to force the social media aspect anyway.

Death of Flash

There were many warnings about the death of Flash before it officially stopped. It was one of the main reasons the site took a hiatus.

2024 Edition

The 2024 Edition made the site modern, so it works in modern browsers and on phones. Flash had been shut down and was just starting to be revived in 2024 with Ruffle. Now the site has Ruffle for Flash games, CheerpJ for Java games and DirPlayer for Shockwave games.

Nov 6, 20122026 Edition

2026 Edition

The 2026 Edition was an overhaul to correct design issues I had for years and finally had ideas and fixes for. The first was the left sidebar, which now has a featured game and navigation tools. The next was game containers on the home page, which let me apply custom themes for holidays. The big holidays I wanted to hit were Halloween and Christmas.

I have always wanted snow to collect on the game containers, and I plan to add that by Christmas of 2027.
